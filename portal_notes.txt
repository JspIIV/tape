Tape gives an agent's on-chain swap an Internet Court ruling on whether it filled at the market price. When an owner says the agent sold badly, nothing on chain can settle it: the minute's market lives on exchanges. Anybody calls rule() with the chain, the swap's hash and the account judged (smart accounts included). GenLayer validators read the receipt and block from two independent RPCs, net the trader's ERC-20 flows in code (fees in the same transaction count), then read that exact minute's candle from OKX and from Coinbase, and rule FAIR or OFF_MARKET against the band, with 100 bps tolerance. No model is asked; integer math only. Unsettled minute, RPCs or venues that disagree, or not a swap for that account: no ruling. Contracts gate on fair(). Proven on Bradbury with real Base swaps: two fair fills, one sold at the pool price but 3% under market after a fee skimmed in the same tx, and a fee taker, a stranger, an unknown hash and an unread chain all refused. 14/14 on chain, 57 checks, source byte-matched.
